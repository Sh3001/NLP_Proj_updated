## 1. Experimental Setup & Blocking Ground Truth

### 1.1 Dataset and Regional Stratification (`data/small`)
The experimental evaluation is conducted on a regional subset (`data/small`, 74 MB) constructed by `src/make_splits.py` using whole-state regional coherence across two diverse countries:
- **United States:** Oregon (`US:OR`) and Utah (`US:UT`) — predominantly English, Latin script, structured address formats with ZIP/ZIP+4 codes.
- **India:** Kerala (`India:Kerala`) and Madhya Pradesh (`India:Madhya Pradesh`) — multilingual, featuring Malayalam and Devanagari scripts alongside English transliterations, and descriptive/unstructured address formats.

### 1.2 Candidate Generation & Blocking Ceiling (`03_candidates`)
Every matcher evaluated in this project operates on the **exact candidate pairs** generated by `03_candidates` (Akshat, Issue #1) via reciprocal-rank fusion (RRF) of character 3-gram sparse cosine similarity and fine-tuned `multilingual-e5-small` bi-encoder dense embeddings ($K_{\text{final}} = 5$).
- **Test Candidate Pairs:** 5 candidates per S2/S3 record.
- **Verified Blocking Ceiling:** The RRF hybrid candidate retrieval achieves an empirical ceiling of **Test Macro $F_{0.5} = 0.99957$** (val ceiling $0.99962$). With high-quality dense retrieval, fewer than $0.05\%$ of true matches fall outside the top-5 candidates.

### 1.3 Strict Data Leakage Prevention (Part A / Part B Partitioning)
To ensure unbiased stacking and calibration:
- Training pairs are split 85% / 15% into **Part A** and **Part B** using deterministic BLAKE2b hashing of S1 entity IDs (`h01(f'AB{SEED}|{o}')`).
- Matchers (`04`, `05a–c`, `06a–f`) are trained exclusively on **Part A**.
- Fusion models in notebook `07` are fitted exclusively on **Part B** (pairs completely unseen by all expert matchers).
- Decision thresholds are tuned strictly on `val`. The held-out `test` split is scored exactly once per model family with test labels unmasked only inside the final scoring evaluator.

## 3. Fusion Methodology: The Five Approaches in Notebook 07

Notebook `07_moe_fusion_and_results` benchmarks five fusion architectures over calibrated matcher logits:

### A. Mean of Calibrated Experts
Each expert matcher's raw logit $z_i$ is calibrated into a probability via univariate logistic regression on Part B:
$$p_i = \sigma(a_i z_i + b_i)$$
The ensemble decision is the unweighted arithmetic mean across all $N$ active experts:
$$p_{\text{mean}}(x) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N p_i(x)$$

### B. Logistic Regression Stacking
A global multivariate logistic regression model trained on Part B logits:
$$p_{\text{LR}}(x) = \sigma\left(\sum_{i=1}^N \beta_i \cdot \frac{z_i - \mu_i}{\sigma_i} + \beta_0\right)$$

### C. Constant-Gate MoE (Ablation Baseline)
An architectural ablation of the Mixture-of-Experts network where gate weights do not receive input features, learning a static parameter vector $w_0 \in \mathbb{R}^N$:
$$w = \text{softmax}(w_0), \quad p(x) = \sigma\left(\sum_{i=1}^N w_i \cdot (a_i z_i + b_i) + c\right)$$

### D. Input-Conditional Gated Mixture of Experts (Our Primary System)
A dynamic gating network conditioning routing weights $w(x)$ on 11 input-level features $g(x) \in \mathbb{R}^{13}$:
1. **Script One-Hot (4):** `[latin, devanagari, malayalam, other_indic]`
2. **Source Indicator (1):** `is_S3` (uncurated source flag)
3. **Address Completeness (2):** `addr_empty`, `addr_has_number`
4. **Normalized Sequence Lengths (2):** `len(name)/40`, `len(addr)/80`
5. **Retrieval Signals (4):** `tf_cos`, `de_cos`, `rrf_rank / K`, `n_cand / K`

$$\tilde{g} = \frac{g - \mu_g}{\sigma_g}$$
$$h = \text{ReLU}(W_1 \tilde{g} + b_1), \quad W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{32 \times 13}$$
$$w(x) = \text{softmax}(W_2 h + b_2), \quad W_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times 32}$$
$$p_{\text{GatedMoE}}(x) = \sigma\left(\sum_{i=1}^N w_i(x) \cdot (a_i z_i + b_i) + c\right)$$

> **CRITICAL DATA-LEAKAGE CONSTRAINT:**
> The gate network **NEVER** receives the country label as an input feature. It routes strictly based on script and data-quality indicators. Country-level distributions are evaluated strictly post-hoc.

### E. LightGBM Stacking
A non-linear tree-based ensemble trained on the concatenated feature vector $[\tilde{Z}, \tilde{G}]$ (expert logits plus gate features) using 400 estimators, learning rate 0.05, and max 31 leaves.
